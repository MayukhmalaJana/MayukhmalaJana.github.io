Research Program

AI methods for tissue morphology, immune phenotyping, and spatial molecular context.

My research focuses on extracting clinically meaningful biomarkers from routine histology and connecting those morphology-derived signals with spatial transcriptomics, immune-cell states, and molecular risk programs.

Virtual immunocyte profiling

Weakly supervised whole-slide image pipelines for CD68+, CD163+ macrophage subtypes and tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte populations from H&E images.

Spatial and multi-omics integration

H&E-derived pathomics feature maps integrated with 10x Visium, Visium HD, Xenium-relevant workflows, gene-expression programs, and molecular risk signatures.

Virtual staining and biomarker inference

IHC and IF-free computational pathology workflows for H&E-to-immune-marker inference and tumor-associated cell phenotyping.

Translational oncology applications

Breast cancer, HPV-associated oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma, ovarian and gynecologic cancers, and multi-organ oncology collaborations.
