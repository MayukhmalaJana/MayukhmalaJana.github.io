Research experience

PhD Researcher | August 2024-present
Madabhushi Lab, Emory University and Georgia Institute of Technology

Develop weakly supervised whole-slide image pipelines for virtual staining, H&E-derived macrophage and lymphocyte profiling, and tumor microenvironment analysis across breast, ovarian, and head and neck cancers.

Integrate histology-derived pathomics with spatial transcriptomics, spatial proteomics, and multi-omics data spanning proteomics, genomics, RNA/transcriptomics, and DNA methylation/epigenomics. Work includes Visium, Visium HD, and Xenium-relevant analyses.

Investigate immune-cell spatial organization, stromal and collagen architecture, and image-derived biomarkers for cancer risk stratification and treatment-response research; broader interests include immune aging and cell senescence.

Contributed data preparation, model development, experiments, validation, survival analysis, and manuscript writing to VISTA (European Journal of Cancer, 2025; second author). First-author VAIP and VIPER abstracts appeared at ASCO 2026 and ESMO 2025.

Collaborate across engineering, pathology, and oncology on study design, manuscripts, conference abstracts, and research proposals, including NIH-aligned proposal preparation.
